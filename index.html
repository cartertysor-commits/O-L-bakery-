Our Story 🥺💕

Why we started O & L Bakery...

"Honestly? It all started during summer break when we stayed up till 3 AM binge-watching baking shows and complaining that there were no good local cookie delivery services for teens who just wanted a sweet treat after a long week of school.

We began experimenting in Olivia's kitchen, ruining two mixers along the way, until we perfected our signature brown-butter chocolate chip recipe. O & L stands for Olivia & Lydia - lifelong friendship turned into our dream small business. Every single batch means the world to us because it's built on late-night laughs, ruined flour-covered countertops, and a shared obsession with warm cookies."
